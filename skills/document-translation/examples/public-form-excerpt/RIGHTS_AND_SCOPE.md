# Source and excerpt scope

This example translates six instruction excerpts from page 1 of a six-page IRS W-9 PDF. It is not a complete translated tax form. The exact selection is recorded in `excerpt-content.json`.

The original PDF is not distributed. Its acquisition URL, hash and test scope are in the [source record](../../references/public-test-provenance.json). `tools/public_pdf_smoke.py` at the repository root can reacquire the test sources. Provider rights apply.
